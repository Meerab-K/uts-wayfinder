About DOFEMINA

DOFEMINA is a group of 4 Artifical Intelligence and Information Technology students at University of Technology (UTS).

We came togethre through our Introduction to Artificial Intelligence (41040) project, sharing an interest in problem-solving, technology, and building practical applications using AI.

Our first project UTS Wayfinder is an AI-based campus route planner designed to help students navigate between buildings.

Our Goal

Our goal is to learn by building.

We want to explore how artificial intelligence can solve real-worls problems while developing our skills in programming, algorithms, teamworks, and software development.
